Retrieved 2026-10-27.

The download (MuzzleFlashVFX.zip) contains no licence file. The licence is stated on the page, under "Licensing:", quoted exactly:

    "You're free to use this pack for personal, educational and commercial projects with no attribution required (CC0)"

Credit is given anyway in the README. The "ExtraTexturesForSupporters" download is a separate paid file and is not included.

Changes made here (permitted by CC0): the files moved under addons/dot_fx/art/binbun_muzzle/, resource paths rewritten to match, the .ptex texture sources and unused placeholder.png dropped, and the two helper scripts' class_name renamed to DotMuzzleVfxController and DotMuzzleVfxLight so they cannot collide with the author's other packs.
